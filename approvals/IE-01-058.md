## Research gaps

- Dunguaire Castle (Dún Guaire) is a tower house on the south-east shore of Galway Bay, just outside Kinvara, County Galway. The name refers to the fort of King Guaire.
- The usual historical account, following John O'Donovan, is that the Ó hEidhin (Hynes) clan built it in 1520. It later passed to the Martyn family.
