## Research gaps

- Clonmacnoise is in County Offaly, on an esker above the Shannon, about 4 km from Shannonbridge. The folder name is the nearby town, as catalogued. The Nun's Church, about 500 metres east, is not in this frame.
- The tall round tower was finished in 1124 and lost its head to lightning in 1135, so the historical tower is flat-topped. A conical cap on that tower in these frames is an interpretation, not a measured profile. Temple Finghin, nearer the river, is the church whose attached round tower still has a conical cap.
- Heritage Ireland states that the original high crosses, including the Cross of the Scriptures, are in the visitor centre, and that replicas stand in the enclosure. These frames show a ringed cross outdoors and do not claim it is the original.
